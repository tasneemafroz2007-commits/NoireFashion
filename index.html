ATELIER HERITAGE

CRAFTING THE PERPETUAL SILHOUETTE

Every NOIRÉ garment begins as an architectural blueprint—a mathematical dialogue between the weight of Italian wool and the fluid motion of the human form.

Founded with a steadfast commitment to slow fashion and uncompromising craftsmanship, our private ateliers in Paris and Milan employ traditional floating canvas construction. We shun mass production in favor of small, numbered editions cut by master artisans.

HOW TO MEASURE ACCURATELY:

  • Bust/Chest: Measure around the fullest part of your chest, keeping the tape level.
  • Waist: Measure around your natural waistline, at the narrowest point.
  • Hips: Stand with feet together and measure around the fullest part of your hips.
